About this guide

On View Paris lists temporary exhibitions at public museums, national and city institutions, and non-profit foundations. It leaves out commercial galleries, permanent collection displays, talks and events.

Each headline names the artist for a solo show, or the subject for a themed one, with the official title beneath it. Free means the exhibition itself is free. Where a date could not be confirmed on a venue's own page, the card says so. Dates change, so check the venue before you travel.

Closed for works at the time of checking: Centre Pompidou, Musée Nissim de Camondo (reopens early 2027) and Musée Yves Saint Laurent Paris (reopens autumn 2027). Musée Cognacq-Jay and Fondation Cartier have no confirmed show running or announced at the time of checking.
