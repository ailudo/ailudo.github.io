      About these listings

      This is an AI project by Studio AI Ludo. Each listing is gathered from the venue's own website and written up with AI, then checked on the date shown at the top. Where a venue publishes only a closing date, the listing reads "Until". Ongoing collection displays, talks and events are left out. Commercial galleries are left out. Headlines are our own, in English: the artist for a solo show, the subject for a thematic show, with the official title (usually French) beneath and the leading artists listed after it. Free means the exhibition itself is free. Where a start date is not published we say “From now until” the end date.

      Closed for works at the time of checking: Centre Pompidou, Musée Nissim de Camondo (reopens early 2027) and Musée Yves Saint Laurent Paris (reopens autumn 2027).
