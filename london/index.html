      About these listings

      This is an AI project by Studio AI Ludo. Each listing is gathered from the venue's own website and written up with AI, then checked on the date shown at the top. Where a venue publishes only a closing date, the listing reads "Until". Ongoing collection displays, talks and events are left out. Headlines are our own: the artist for a solo show, the subject for a thematic show, with the official title beneath and the leading artists listed after it.

      Opening hours, prices and booking change. Each card links to the venue on Google Maps, where its current opening hours are shown. Check before you travel.

      华语 Sinophone

      Marks a solo show by an artist from the Chinese-speaking world (mainland China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Macau and the diaspora), or a group show built around such artists. "Includes" marks a group show with at least one such artist. Tags are editorial.
